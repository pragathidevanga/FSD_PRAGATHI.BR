About Chikmagalur

Chikmagalur is a picturesque hill station located in the Western Ghats of Karnataka. Known as the "Coffee Land of Karnataka," it is famous for its sprawling coffee plantations, lush green hills, and cool climate. Mullayanagiri, the highest peak in Karnataka, offers breathtaking panoramic views and trekking opportunities. Baba Budangiri is a historical and scenic hill famous for its shrine and stunning landscapes. Kudremukh National Park is a biodiversity hotspot with rolling hills, dense forests, and abundant wildlife. Waterfalls like Hebbe Falls, Kalhatti Falls, and Jhari Falls become spectacular during the monsoon season. Hirekolale Lake and viewpoints like Z Point offer serene spots for nature lovers and photographers. Overall, Chikmagalur is a perfect destination for trekking, coffee tours, photography, and peaceful retreats in nature. Chikmagalur is a beautiful hill station located in the Western Ghats of Karnataka. It is well known for its lush green coffee plantations, mist-covered hills, and pleasant climate throughout the year. The town offers a peaceful escape from busy city life and is a favorite destination for nature lovers, photographers, and adventure seekers. One of the major attractions of Chikmagalur is Mullayanagiri, the highest peak in Karnataka, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding valleys and clouds. Nearby places like Baba Budangiri are famous for their scenic beauty and historical significance. Kudremukh National Park is another highlight, known for its rich biodiversity, trekking trails, and rolling green landscapes. Chikmagalur is also famous for its beautiful waterfalls such as Hebbe Falls, Jhari Falls, Kalhatti Falls, and Manikyadhara Falls, especially during the monsoon season when they are at their best. Hirekolale Lake and viewpoints like Z Point provide calm and picturesque spots to relax and enjoy nature. Overall, Chikmagalur is a perfect destination for those who love nature, adventure, calm surroundings, and scenic views, making it one of the most charming tourist places in Karnataka.
